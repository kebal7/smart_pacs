# Smart PACS & Treatment Management
Streamlining radiology workflows and treatment processes.

---

## Overview
This project centralizes medical imaging (PACS) and treatment workflows to make radiologists' jobs easier and improve patient care.  
Key objectives:

- Centralized PACS for imaging storage and retrieval.
- Radiographer portal for faster image acquisition and upload.
- Radiologist portal with AI suggestions to assist interpretation.
- Clinician portal for linking imaging to patient treatment cases.
- Streamlined treatment workflow for doctors, nurses, and specialists.

---

## Features / Modules

### Core PACS
- **Radiographer portal:** upload images and fill patient metadata.
- **Radiologist portal:** view images, write reports, AI suggestions.
- **Clinician imaging view:** access reports and images.

### Treatment Management
- **Case creation and assignment.**
- **Expert collaboration and commenting.**
- **Daily logs and timeline view.**

### Bridge Service
- Syncs patient/study metadata between Core PACS and Treatment Management.
